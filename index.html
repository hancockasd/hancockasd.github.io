Selected work

Conversational self-service assistant

Project lead & project manager · in progress

A large share of inbound requests were repeat questions already answered somewhere in the documentation. Users could not find the answer; agents re-typed it. Volume grew with the user base; headcount did not.

I clustered a year of ticket history to work out which questions were actually deflectable, then defined the product scope for an assistant built on the company's Joule platform and rolled out to the global partner base. I agreed the staged release plan with engineering, set up an evaluation set so answer quality could be measured rather than argued about, and ran fortnightly feedback rounds with internal service teams and partner users.

It is live and iterating. Each release is judged on deflection rate and answer accuracy, not on features shipped, and the backlog is re-prioritised from real usage data every cycle.

RAG draft-reply pipeline

Designer & builder · in production

Agents spent most of their handling time locating the right reference document and rewriting the same explanation. The response SLA was under constant pressure for reasons that had nothing to do with how hard the questions were.

I built an automation that takes an incoming ticket, retrieves the relevant documents from a vector database, drafts a contextual reply, and then stops — holding the draft for agent review before anything is sent. Human approval was a deliberate design constraint rather than a limitation; it is what made the team willing to use it at all.

Drafting went from a manual research task to a sub-minute step, and the average first response settled under an hour across 3,000+ annual requests.

Agent-side AI assist in the live chat console

Designer & builder · deployed across APAC

A multilingual APAC team answering a global user base in English: inconsistent tone, slow message editing, and a constant context-switch to a translation tab.

Rather than propose a platform migration, I shipped a browser extension that injects a polish action and an inline translation control directly into the chat console the agents already use, backed by a small service calling the enterprise AI gateway. Less editing per conversation, and noticeably more consistent replies across the region.

Automated reporting pipeline

Designer & builder · SAP, and earlier at Xiamen Air

Recurring operational reporting was assembled by hand each cycle — slow, and wrong often enough that stakeholders had quietly stopped trusting it.

I replaced it with an ELT pipeline in Python: extraction, AI-assisted analysis, automated publishing to Power BI, callable as a routine step rather than a monthly project. It now covers 85% of weekly reporting, with report latency down 40% and errors down 32%. The same approach had earlier replaced a spreadsheet-based sales reporting cycle at Xiamen Air, cutting data errors by 86%, from 5.3% to 0.8%.
